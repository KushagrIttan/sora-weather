# Sora anime artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Final assets are bundled in `assets/weather/` and used by `AnimeWeatherCard`. These are stylized illustrations, not photographs of the selected city.

Shared prompt: Production portrait 2:3 anime environment background for a modern mobile weather app. Cinematic contemporary anime environmental concept art, hand-painted detail, delicate linework and painterly cel shading. Luminous pastel color design. Top 45% mostly atmospheric sky for typography; scenery below. Full bleed, no UI, text, logos, borders, flat vector shapes or existing franchise.

## clear.png

A brilliant blue sky full of towering luminous cumulus clouds over a sunlit Japanese seaside town, pastel pink hydrangeas and a bicycle in the foreground, sparkling turquoise ocean, soft peach rooftops and an airy sense of summer freedom. Bright midday, unmistakably clear pleasant weather.

Palette: sky blue, butter yellow, blush pink and turquoise.

## rain.png

A rainy Japanese neighborhood seen from a covered balcony, translucent pastel pink umbrella leaning beside potted flowers, blue hydrangeas, glistening street puddles reflecting lavender buildings, distant layered rain clouds and visible delicate rain streaks. Rich luminous rainy atmosphere, no sun.

Palette: periwinkle, lavender, ice blue and muted rose.

## snow.png

A magical snowy Japanese neighborhood with powdery fresh snow on roofs, a cozy tiny cafe with amber windows, snow-laden cherry tree branches framing the foreground, distant snow mountains, visible delicate falling snowflakes under a pale pastel blue winter sky. Clear sense of cold snow.

Palette: ice blue, soft lilac, blush and warm peach.

## night.png

A luminous Japanese seaside neighborhood at night, a huge sweeping starry indigo sky with a luminous crescent moon, fluffy lavender clouds, warm glowing windows, distant sea reflecting moonlight, subtle foreground flowers and power lines. Dreamy but crisp nighttime atmosphere.

Palette: periwinkle blue, lavender, indigo, glowing peach and mint.

## cloudy.png

Overcast Japanese seaside neighborhood, thick silver-lavender cloud cover, mint sea, pastel peach rooftops, pink hydrangeas and an aqua bicycle beside steps. Luminous diffuse daytime light, no sun or clear blue sky. Powder blue, lilac, mint and blush pink. Same shared style and composition constraints.
